# Product definition

Updated: 2026-10-24. Canonical owner requirements and working direction. Current delivery state: [STATUS.md](STATUS.md). Proposed interactions: [EXPERIENCE.md](EXPERIENCE.md).

## What Biz Da Vinci is

A service helping local-business owners understand an existing website, explore a better design and understand likely improvement costs. Owners can use the free advice themselves or choose managed implementation.

Working positioning, **proposed**:

> See what your business website could do better—and what it would take to improve it.

The intended business outcomes vary by profession: calls, appointments, quote requests, visits or orders. These are goals to investigate, not promised results.

## Recommended product model — proposed

Three connected routes: **What should I fix?** → audit; **How could it look?** → redesign concept; **What would it cost?** → estimate. Each can stand alone and reuse the business context from another route.

Offer a useful public-site review, a bounded homepage concept and a transparent estimate for free. Paid continuation can include targeted fixes, full redesign, content, integrations, migration and optional care. A rebuild should follow need, not be the automatic recommendation for every business.

The proposed distinction is the combination of audit, concept and estimate before contact details. Research found competitors already offering ungated calculators, so free estimating alone is not a uniqueness claim.

## Boundaries

Managed website improvement remains the service theme. C12 explicitly permits a free DIY prompt option alongside managed services; it supersedes C2 only for that resource. Do not position the whole service as a DIY builder, restaurant-only product or a guaranteed source of customers. Do not automatically add voice agents, CRM, outreach automation or a large agency service catalog to the first release. Those capabilities require their own task and validation.

The implemented visual direction builds on the owner's estimate-screen reference and Mobbin-informed gallery/comparison patterns. The owner authorized palette changes and requested complete, clean comparison imagery and improved scrolling. Current visual tokens live in [DESIGN.md](../frontend/DESIGN.md). This remains a reviewable local design, not a final production approval. Public prices, delivery economics, production stack and launch date remain open; see [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).
