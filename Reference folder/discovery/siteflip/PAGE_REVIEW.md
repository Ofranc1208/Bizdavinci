# siteflip: page-by-page review

> Reference evidence (2026-10-23). For current product decisions and instructions, start with [the workspace documentation](../../../README.md). Observations, recommendations and vendor claims retain the qualifications below.

Reviewed October 23, 2026. 4 selected public pages. Each entry records retrieved content, a design judgment and a proposed Biz Da Vinci response. These are not measured conversion findings. Long testimonial collections, indexes and legal/FAQ pages were reviewed selectively as specified below. See [method](../METHOD.md), [site synthesis](README.md), [coverage](coverage.json) and [retrieval metadata](retrieval-metadata.json). Browser testing is limited to the states documented in the site synthesis.

## 1. Homepage and request journey

[Source page](https://www.siteflip.app/)

**Observed:** A focused offer moves from an illustrated before/after to preview, plans, FAQs and a URL/email request. The request promises delivery within 48 hours.

**What works:** The visual transformation and preview-before-purchase sequence make the service concrete.

**Friction or uncertainty:** The opening implies prior redesign work, while the generic form requests future work. The result is delayed and contact-led; illustrated improvements are not measured prospect outcomes.

**Biz Da Vinci implication:** Use a clearly labeled on-screen homepage concept and explain each proposed improvement. Put delivery timing, limits and ownership beside the offer.

## 2. Terms

[Source page](https://www.siteflip.app/terms)

**Observed:** Distinguishes a no-obligation preview from paid delivery, change allowances and subscription licensing.

**What works:** Scope and cancellation provisions make the commercial relationship more inspectable.

**Friction or uncertainty:** Subscription source acquisition carries a separately agreed exit fee. Readers could interpret homepage export language more broadly.

**Biz Da Vinci implication:** Explain exactly what the customer owns, receives and can export; give cancellation costs before purchase.

## 3. Privacy

[Source page](https://www.siteflip.app/privacy)

**Observed:** Describes URL collection, voluntarily supplied information, providers and retention.

**What works:** A specific policy is more useful than a generic trust badge.

**Friction or uncertainty:** The visible email request is not clearly reconciled with the URL-focused description. Submission validation was not tested.

**Biz Da Vinci implication:** Use the same field definitions in product and privacy copy; separate optional report delivery from marketing permission.

## 4. Legal notice

[Source page](https://www.siteflip.app/legal)

**Observed:** Provides an identifiable operator and business contact in Germany.

**What works:** An accountable business identity supports trust.

**Friction or uncertainty:** Identity details do not validate performance or customer claims.

**Biz Da Vinci implication:** Publish actual Biz Da Vinci business details and contact channels; obtain appropriate original terms rather than copying another operator's text.
