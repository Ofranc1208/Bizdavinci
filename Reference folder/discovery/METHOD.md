# Research method and evidence boundaries

> Reference evidence (2026-10-23). For current product decisions and instructions, start with [the workspace documentation](../../README.md). Observations, recommendations and vendor claims retain the qualifications below.

Research date: October 23, 2026. Purpose: inform the Biz Da Vinci website and service proposition for multiple local-business professions.

## What was done

- Enumerated public URLs using Firecrawl maps, with query parameters ignored and a 300-result cap per map.
- Retrieved selected public buying-journey pages live (`maxAge: 0`), covering the primary offer, pricing, free tools, service delivery, industry relevance, case evidence and terms.
- Read and compared relevant page sections. Long review widgets and broad portfolio indexes were sampled for presentation structure, not independently verified testimonial by testimonial.
- Inspected key rendered flows with the in-app browser and saved screenshots. Screenshots used in findings were reopened/viewed after saving.
- Executed one calculator scenario each at Pronto and WebFX without entering personal/contact data or sending a sales request.
- Recorded observations separately from recommendations and unverified vendor claims.

## Coverage vocabulary

**Discovered** means a map returned the URL. It does not mean its content was read.

**Content reviewed** means retrieved page content informed a page-specific note. It does not mean every link, video, carousel, embedded review or account flow was tested.

**Browser verified** means a particular visible state or action was inspected. It does not imply a complete cross-device, accessibility, performance or backend audit.

**Not reviewed** means a discovered URL was not part of the selected detailed customer journey. The coverage register retains these URLs rather than implying all were covered.

The larger sites contain extensive article, portfolio, location and archive collections. This work is a deep review of selected decision-making paths, not exhaustive inspection of every public URL across all five domains. The WebFX map hit its cap and is explicitly incomplete. Some maps omit homepages or return aliases; canonical redirects are recorded in retrieval metadata.

## What was not done

No competitor lead form was submitted, no account created, no call booked, no SMS consent given, no payment made, and no client data from the Tampa workbook sent to these sites. No delivered paid service was evaluated. No claims of conversion lift were independently established.

The browser viewport was approximately 914×943 pixels. We did not perform a systematic phone/desktop breakpoint test, screen-reader audit, Lighthouse measurement, search-ranking experiment or security assessment. Apparent extraction artifacts are not reported as visible defects unless independently observed in the browser.

## Sources and traceability

Each site folder has a report, page register, URL inventory, coverage JSON, retrieval metadata and screenshots where inspected. Metadata includes requested URL, returned URL/title, status and scrape identifier where supplied. Reports paraphrase findings rather than republish complete competitor pages. Prices are dated observations, may vary by scope, and are not approved Biz Da Vinci prices.

The Product Design audit workflow was used for visible-flow evidence. Firecrawl supplied URL discovery and page retrieval. Native application control was not needed.
